# Mythweave Wilds image-generation prompt set

Generation mode: built-in image generation. Each atlas is a 4×4 row-major sprite sheet on a flat magenta chroma-key background, with one isolated subject per equal cell, no text, no borders, no scenery, and no shadows crossing cell boundaries. Art direction is premium hand-authored pixel art: crisp silhouette, deliberate clusters, dark selective outlines, soft internal highlights, luminous accents, and no ordinary-animal recolor families.

## Form II prompt

Create the sixteen original adult mythical fur-bearing species above. Every silhouette and face system must be unmistakably different. Make them feel like fauna from one impossible ecosystem, not hybrids of familiar Earth animals. Preserve the exact species order.

## Form I prompt

Create younger hatchling forms of the same sixteen species in the same order. Preserve each lineage's defining topology, face system, sensors, and signature appendage, but simplify anatomy, shorten extremities, enlarge the head slightly, and reduce ornaments. They must be recognizable juveniles, not chibi recolors or eggs.

## Form III prompt

Create mature evolved forms of the same sixteen species in the same order. Preserve lineage identity and palette while changing anatomy meaningfully: expand halos or portals, add articulated limbs or mantle structures where appropriate, strengthen signature organs, and make silhouettes more authoritative. Do not merely enlarge or recolor Form II.

## Egg prompt

Create sixteen distinct unhatched forms in the same order. Each must encode its lineage through shell topology, aperture or eye motif, crown/sensor clue, appendage seed, and palette. Forms may be eggs, cocoons, floating seed-vessels, or sealed dream pods, but must read as dormant and hatchable. Do not place adult creatures inside the eggs.
