## Form prompts

- **Form II:** Create the established adult versions in the exact order. Elements must be biologically embodied rather than added as icons, costumes, or handheld powers.
- **Form I:** Preserve each lineage's body, face, elemental organ, palette, and signature appendage while simplifying anatomy and reducing orbiting or crown structures. Subjects are juveniles, not eggs or miniatures.
- **Form III:** Preserve lineage identity while substantially expanding elemental organs, articulated structures, locomotion, portals, halos, and crowns. Never merely enlarge, recolor, costume, or arm Form II.
- **Egg:** Encode every lineage through dormant shell topology, aperture, elemental organ, sensor clue, appendage seed, and palette. Ember vessels, tide pods, sealed geodes, cocoons, and cloud seeds are allowed, but all must read as unhatched.
